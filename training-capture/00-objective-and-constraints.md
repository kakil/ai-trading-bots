# Objective and constraints

## Project
AI Trading Bots: a free, no-code course.

## Baseline date
2026-11-14

## Objective
Teach beginners to build, backtest and supervise a TradingView trading bot with Claude, without writing code themselves.

## Course topics
- Connecting Claude to TradingView through the TradingView MCP
- 5 rules for bots
- The Turtle breakout strategy, written in Pine Script by Claude
- Backtesting in TradingView
- Alerts
- Oversight rules: how a person supervises the bot

## Audience
People who don't code. Some may be new to trading too.

## Constraints
- **No-code for the learner.** Claude writes the Pine Script. The learner copies, runs and checks it.
- **Education, not financial advice.** No profit promises and no unverified performance numbers.
- **Paper trading first.** The course doesn't walk anyone into live, funded trading.
- **Free.** No paid tier is planned as part of this repository.
- **No secrets or personal account details** in the repo or in screenshots.

## Success looks like
- A beginner can finish the course and have a working, backtested Turtle strategy with alerts on their own TradingView chart.
- They know the rules for supervising it.
- Every lesson can be traced back to real, recorded work in this folder.
